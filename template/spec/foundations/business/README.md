**Business holds what we decided** about the product as a business: why it exists, who it is for,
what is free and what is paid, how success is measured. What we measured outside goes in
[Market](../market/README.md).

**Empty.** No business document is written yet ([`ENT-02`](https://github.com/openpedd/openpedd/blob/main/spec/foundations/domain/foundation.md)).

| Document | ID | Holds |
|---|---|---|

## What a product's Business covers

| Document | Holds |
|---|---|
| Problem and goal | What the product solves, for whom, what it does not solve |
| Audience | Who it is sold to (ICP), the personas who use it |
| Pricing and plans | What is charged, how, and for what. Private if the spec is public |
| North-star metric | The one number every module metric hangs from |
| Positioning | Why it is chosen over the alternatives in Market |

## How a business document is written

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| It states the decision and its reason, in the present | [`RULE-02`](https://github.com/openpedd/openpedd/blob/main/spec/foundations/rules/writing.md#rule-02) |
| A figure from outside is cited from Market, not copied | One thing, one place |
| A number that governs (a price, a limit) is written once, here | Modules reference it |
| Budget: 2,000 tokens | [`RULE-04`](https://github.com/openpedd/openpedd/blob/main/spec/foundations/rules/writing.md#rule-04) |
