**Marketing holds how the product speaks to people who do not know it yet.** It is optional: a
product with no marketing leaves it empty. It is different from the neighbours it is easy to
confuse it with:

| Foundation | Speaks to | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| `Voice & Tone` | Users, **inside** the product | How the app, the emails and the help sound |
| **Marketing** | People **outside**, who do not know it yet | What we say, where, and the rules of each format |
| Business | The team | What we decided: audience, pricing, positioning |
| Market | The team | What we measured outside |

**Empty.** No marketing document is written yet ([`ENT-02`](https://github.com/openpedd/openpedd/blob/main/spec/foundations/domain/foundation.md)).

## What it covers

| Document | Holds |
|---|---|
| Messages | The promise, the proof points, what we never claim. Built on the positioning in Business |
| Channels | Where we speak (site, social networks, email, events) and to whom in each |
| Formats | The rules of each piece: a landing section, a post, a carousel, a cold email |
| Calendar | The cadence of each channel |

## Rules

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| **The rules are spec; the pieces are not.** A post, an ad or a carousel lives in the marketing team's own repo or tool | Pieces are many and short-lived; the rules that make them consistent are few and last |
| A claim about the product cites the requirement that makes it true | Marketing cannot promise what the spec does not say |
| A figure from outside is cited from Market | One thing, one place |
| The words on screen come from the glossary | A feature has one name, inside and outside |
| Budget: 2,000 tokens | [`RULE-04`](https://github.com/openpedd/openpedd/blob/main/spec/foundations/rules/writing.md#rule-04) |
