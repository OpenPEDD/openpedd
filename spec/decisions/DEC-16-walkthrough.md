# DEC-16 · Guided help on request is called a Walkthrough

**Date:** 2026-11-11

The artifact is a `Walkthrough`. What a product shows on the first visit is Onboarding. The detail is
in [`ARCH-07`](../foundations/architecture/walkthroughs.md).

**Why:** a tour usually covers a product in general and is pushed at the user; this covers one task
and the person asks for it. `Guide` and `Flow` collide with names already in use.
