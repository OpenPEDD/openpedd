# DEC-20 · OpenPEDD is the brand; the free framework is OpenPEDD Framework

**Date:** 2026-11-19 · **Supersedes:** the framework's name in [`DEC-01`](DEC-01-name.md)

| What | Name |
|---|---|
| The brand, and the family of products under it | **OpenPEDD** |
| The open framework: format, CLI, skills, judges, local MCP, reader, CI action | **OpenPEDD Framework** |
| The paid platform | **OpenPEDD Cloud** |
| The methodology and the CLI | Unchanged: **PEDD** and **`pedd`** |

In running text, **OpenPEDD alone means the framework** when no other product is in play. Where the
Framework and the Cloud are compared, or a reader could take one for the other, the full names are
used.

**Why:** OpenPEDD carries more than one product. With the framework named apart, "OpenPEDD" can name
the whole family, and a paid product can sit beside the free one without anyone reading the free one
as a trial.
