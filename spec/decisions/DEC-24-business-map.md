# DEC-24 · A business is mapped before its system is designed

**Date:** 2026-11-20

**A product that changes how an organization works starts with a `Business Map`**: how it works today
(as-is) and how it will work with the system (to-be), drawn by a product engineer and approved by the
client. It is optional for any other product. The foundation is in
[`ENT-02`](../foundations/domain/foundation.md); how it is drawn is `map`'s business mode
([map](../modules/map/prd.md#business-mode)).

**Why:** a conversation with the client gives what they ask for, not how their people, processes and
spreadsheets fit together; a system designed without that automates the wrong process
([`MKT-11`](../foundations/market/business-mapping.md)).
