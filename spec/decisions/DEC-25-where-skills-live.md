# DEC-25 · The core holds the method; everything else is an extension or the Cloud

**Date:** 2026-11-20

| Kind | Holds | Where | Paid |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Core** | The method: the stages, their judges, the interview, `map` | `openpedd/openpedd` | No |
| **Extension** | Any other skill, ours or found online (a logo, a deck), including one that calls a service **with the team's own key**, on request | The [extensions catalog](../foundations/business/open-core.md#extensions), license checked | No |
| **Cloud add-on** | What OpenPEDD hosts, or pays the key for: image generation, a template gallery | OpenPEDD Cloud | Yes |

**Why:** it is the line of [`BIZ-04`](../foundations/business/open-core.md): what runs when you ask is
free. A key is not what makes something paid; a service OpenPEDD runs or pays for is.
