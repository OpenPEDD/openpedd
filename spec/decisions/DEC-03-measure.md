# DEC-03 · `measure`: the stage is free, automation is paid

**Closes:** `Q-03` · **Date:** 2026-11-04

| Free | OpenPEDD Cloud |
|---|---|
| The `/measure` skill, the PRD's metric section, the `Tracking Plan` | Analytics connectors (PostHog, Amplitude, SQL) |
| The person enters the value or configures the query, and runs `/measure` on the date | It runs on the review date, notifies, and opens the `Q` |

**Why:** it is the open-core line of [`BIZ-04`](../foundations/business/open-core.md): what runs when
you ask is free; what watches for you is paid.
