# DEC-02 · Licenses

**Closes:** `Q-02` · **Date:** 2026-11-04

| Piece | License | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The format (spec structure, deltas, anchors, `spec-graph.json`) and the framework's docs | **CC BY 4.0** | We want it adopted as a standard, even by others: the format is the network effect |
| The framework: CLI, skills, agents, local MCP, reader, CI action | **Apache-2.0** | Like MIT, plus a patent grant and no trademark grant. Companies accept it without legal review |
| OpenPEDD Cloud: sync engine, dashboard, two-way tracker, judges as a service, hosted MCP | **Closed** | It can open later under FSL; what opens never closes again |
| The name and logo | **Registered trademark**, classes 9 and 42, at INPI and USPTO or through the Madrid Protocol | The real protection of an open core: code can be forked, the name cannot |
| Outside contributions | **CLA**, through CLA Assistant, on the Apache ICLA model | Without it, nothing can be relicensed or moved from the framework to the Cloud |

**Not used:** MIT (Apache gives the same plus patents), AGPL (keeps away the companies that later
pay), BSL or SSPL on the framework (no longer open source), Commons Clause (ambiguous).

This is not legal advice: a lawyer reviews the CLA and the trademark filing before anything is
published.
