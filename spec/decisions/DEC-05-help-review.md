# DEC-05 · How the Help Center is published

**Closes:** `Q-05` · **Date:** 2026-11-04

| Case | Review |
|---|---|
| A **new** article | A person, the first time |
| A change to an existing article | **Ships on its own** when the `freshness-judge` passes |

Configurable with `publish.help_review: new_only | always | never`. Default: `new_only`.

**Why:** the copy already went through a person in `specify` and `design`. What the first review
checks is the article's structure.
